Three changes every Bangladeshi company is facing now.

LDC graduation is three years away

The UN General Assembly moved the date from 2026 to 2029. Exporters now have three years to cut cost and errors before trade benefits start to change.

Prothom Alo, 30 Oct 2026

Online VAT

VAT returns are moving online

NBR now requires online returns from sectors including medicines, cement, banks and insurers, and plans to extend this to every VAT-paying business. Clean digital records are no longer optional.

VATupdate, Sep 2026

Data law

Personal data now has its own law

The Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 sets duties for anyone handling staff and customer records. Knowing where your files live is now part of running a company.
